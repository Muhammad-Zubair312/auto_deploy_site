My Journey in Tech

My career began with a Computer Science degree and a fascination for how pixels on a screen could solve real human problems. I started as a junior developer at a digital agency, where I cut my teeth on everything from responsive brochure sites to complex e-commerce platforms.

Over the years, I've specialized in the React ecosystem and modern CSS frameworks like Tailwind, focusing on creating component libraries and design systems that scale. I believe great front-end development is equal parts art and engineering—meticulous attention to detail paired with clean, maintainable code.

Currently, I lead the front-end practice at Nexus Digital Solutions, where I mentor a team of 5 developers and collaborate closely with product and design to ship features used by millions of monthly active users.

Outside of client work, I'm an active contributor to open-source projects, a occasional conference speaker, and I write about modern web development practices on my blog.
