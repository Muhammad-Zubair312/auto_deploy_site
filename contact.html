Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with me.

1 What is your typical project timeline?

Most projects range from 4-12 weeks depending on scope. A standard responsive website with 5-7 pages typically takes 6-8 weeks from kickoff to launch. I provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

2 Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! I work with clients worldwide. My primary working hours are PST, but I'm flexible for important meetings across time zones. All communication is in English, and I accept payments in multiple currencies.

3 What is your pricing structure?

I offer both fixed-price projects for well-defined scopes and hourly rates for ongoing work or consultations. Most projects start at $5,000 for basic websites, with custom web applications ranging from $15,000-$50,000+. I provide detailed proposals after our initial discussion.

4 Do you provide ongoing maintenance after launch?

Yes, I offer monthly maintenance plans starting at $200/month. These include security updates, performance monitoring, content updates, and technical support. Many clients choose to retain me for 3-6 months post-launch to ensure everything runs smoothly.
