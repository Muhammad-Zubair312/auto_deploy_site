From a Simple Idea to a Global Network

Founded in 2018 by former Olympic coach Dr. Anya Sharma and sports technologist Marcus Chen, Sprot began as a passion project to digitize training logs. We quickly realized athletes needed more: structured education, verified coaching, and a way to connect their progress to real-world opportunities.

Today, Sprot is the leading integrated platform for sports education. We combine scientifically-backed training plans, in-depth athlete analytics, and a vibrant community for sharing achievements. We're not just tracking workouts; we're building smarter, more resilient athletes.

  • Serving 250,000+ athletes across 15 sports disciplines
  • Partnered with 50+ national federations and universities
  • Featured in SportsTech Review's "Top 10 Innovators" for 3 consecutive years
