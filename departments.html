Role-Based Department Access

Access to department information and patient records is controlled based on user roles to ensure HIPAA compliance and data security.

Doctors

  • Full access to assigned department
  • View patient medical records
  • Update treatment plans
  • Limited access to other departments

Medical Staff

  • Access to assigned department
  • Update patient vitals
  • Cannot modify treatment plans
  • No access to billing data

Patients

  • View department information
  • Access own medical records
  • Cannot view other patient data
  • No access to staff schedules
