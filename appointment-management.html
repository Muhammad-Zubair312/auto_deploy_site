All appointment data is protected under HIPAA regulations. Access to patient information is restricted based on role permissions. Unauthorized access or disclosure is prohibited.

Role-Based Access Control

Patient Access

  • View own appointments
  • Request appointment changes
  • Cannot view other patients
  • No access to medical records

Doctor Access

  • View assigned appointments
  • Update appointment status
  • Access patient medical history
  • Cannot modify billing

Staff/Admin Access

  • Full appointment management
  • Schedule and reschedule
  • Access all patient data
  • Billing integration
